The approach is smart. They used task-specific LLMs to extract narrative-related features out of LLM texts (yes, they used LLMs to help detect LLMs). They concatanate these answers together, forming a "feature vector" for each text. They essentially created structured representations from texts, a "fingerprint", that subsequent tools can now analyze downstream.
