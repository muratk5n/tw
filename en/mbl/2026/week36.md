way natural gas is today. Approximately 1,600 miles of hydrogen

hydrogen users, such as petroleum refineries and chemical plants, are

option for delivering large volumes of hydrogen.. Research today
therefore focuses on overcoming technical concerns related to pipeline
transmission..  Potential solutions include using fiber reinforced
polymer (FRP) pipelines for hydrogen distribution. The installation

pipelines because the FRP can be obtained in sections that are much
longer than steel minimizing welding requirements."
