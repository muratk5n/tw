“To calculate the logarithm of 333 with base 2 (\(\log_2(333)\)), you can use the change of base formula:

\[ \log_2(333) = \frac{\ln(333)}{\ln(2)} \]

Calculation: 1. \(\ln(333) \approx 5.80814\). \(\ln(2) \approx 0.69315\). \(5.80814 \div 0.69315 \approx 8.37937\)

Result: \[ \log_2(333) \approx 8.3794 \]

Context: This makes sense because \(2^8 = 256\) and \(2^9 = 512\). Since 333 is between 256 and 512, the result must be between 8 and 9.”
