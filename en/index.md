Aalbers makes a good point; investors will go after *existing* homes,
they won't finance building of new homes. I see a lot of commentators
make this mistake, they look at housing demand on a headcount basis,
then housing construction and try to gauge some kind of shortfall, or
balance. What they really need to look at is *money* flowing into
housing, and the housing stock. Does the growth of the stock correlate
with the amount of money going in?  If not, why not?
